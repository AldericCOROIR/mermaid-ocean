# Castle paint-overs, v3

Built-in ImageGen edited the exact 1280×720 clean game screenshots. In both
prompts, image 1 was the game screenshot and image 2 was
`castle_overview_concept.png`. The runtime uses JPEG copies of the final PNGs.

## Gate

Use case: style-transfer. Asset type: paint-over projection texture for a Three.js underwater fairy-tale castle, landscape 16:9. Image 1 is the exact game camera screenshot and strict geometry/composition target. Paint over it without changing camera, silhouette, wall/tower/gate positions, openings, ground horizon, or perspective. Image 2 is the design and painterly color/style reference, especially its shell portal and ivory-pink-aqua-gold architecture. Transform the plain screenshot into richly hand-painted storybook gouache: ribbed pink and aqua clam-shell gate leaves standing open around the real central passage, ornate gold traceries on the arch and towers, luminous pearl windows, delicate shell reliefs on the curved curtain wall, coherent soft underwater light and blue depth. Keep the central passage wide and visibly open, with the distant palace seen through it. Preserve every large shape and boundary from Image 1 so this painting projects correctly back onto the 3D geometry. No people, no text, no labels, no UI, no frame.

Background: grand ivory palace with pink ribbed shell dome, tall aqua and lilac shell towers, luminous arched windows, ornate gold tracery and a large open doorway. Do not move or add major structures beyond Image 1, do not close the doorway, no characters, text, UI, labels or frame.
